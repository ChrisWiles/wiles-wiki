# AI infrastructure research baseline — October 14, 2026

**Cutoff:** October 14, 2026, America/Chicago. **Status:** partial verification baseline; not a completed weekly literature sweep. No prior committed report exists for comparison. This establishes the claims to monitor rather than presenting older research as new this week.

The central thesis remains a testable scenario: useful AI workloads could expand faster than efficiency reduces resources per task, while physical infrastructure creates opportunities for suppliers with durable economics. Evidence of research acceleration strengthens the capability side; it does not establish AGI timing, unlimited profitable inference demand or attractive valuations for every supplier.

## Implications to test in company research

- Power equipment/cooling: demand and delivery constraints can matter even without a fixed AGI date; test order quality, cancellations, production capacity, margins and valuation.
- Semiconductors/memory/networking: follow qualified supply and architecture substitution, not merely announced spending. Test scarcity's duration and who captures margins.
- Compute operators: distinguish revenue growth from cash returns after replacement spending, borrowing costs and dilution. Falling inference prices can expand usage while compressing operator returns.
- Efficiency: falling API prices are not a direct physical-compute savings measure. Both workload growth and resources per comparable workload must be measured.
