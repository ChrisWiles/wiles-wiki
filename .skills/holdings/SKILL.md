# Holdings

Read ignored `references/accounts.md` to select the requested account and its position file. Keep accounts separate unless a combined view is requested.

Store only tickers and share counts. For options, store contract and count; for Treasury notes, issue and face amount. Leave missing quantities unknown. Keep GOOG and GOOGL separate. Keep updates concise: no screenshots, source notes, prices, gains, percentages or transcription commentary.

Account references are private and Git-ignored. In an isolated report worktree, read them from the source checkout. Never publish the account map, positions or account-level calculations; use them only for requested personal analysis. A research report does not update positions automatically.
