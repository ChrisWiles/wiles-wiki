# Holdings

Read `references/accounts.md` to select the requested account and its position file. Keep accounts separate unless a combined view is requested.

Store only tickers and share counts. For options, store contract and count; for Treasury notes, issue and face amount. Leave missing quantities unknown. Keep GOOG and GOOGL separate. Keep updates concise: no screenshots, source notes, prices, gains, percentages or transcription commentary.

Tax-lot dates, basis and holding-period detail belong in the separate input ledger described by `investing/tax-review.md`; do not add them to these minimal position references. Position totals alone do not establish acquisition dates.

The user authorizes publishing household account labels, holdings and allocation analysis in this repository. Read the committed references in the report worktree; prefer a newer user-updated source-checkout record when available. Reports may include these positions and account-level calculations. Leave missing inputs unknown. A research report does not update positions automatically.
