# Weekly investing research

Two repo-local skills run sequentially every Saturday at 9 AM America/Chicago:

1. `ai-research-monitor` refreshes primary AI research and the infrastructure thesis.
2. `stock-analysis` refreshes company economics and conditional swing setups.

They compare against prior state, create dated Markdown reports, and commit/push only their report and state changes under the [publication protocol](publication.md). [Reports](../reports/investing/index.md) are public; personal holdings and account calculations stay local.

The [mandate](mandate.md) defines the 2026–2035 thesis, research universe, uncertainty rules and separation of long-term investing from swing trading. [Sources](research-sources.md) are starting points, not a whitelist or proof that seed claims are true.

`holdings` remains the private position record. Generic investing skills support deeper work when needed; weekly runs do not require every installed skill or a paid broker integration.
