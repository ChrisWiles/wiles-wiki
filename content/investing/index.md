**[Open the visual report archive →](https://chriswiles.github.io/wiles-wiki/investing-notebook/)**

Each edition brings together the AI research, stock review and available portfolio projections. Explore forecast ranges, switch between six months and one through five years, compare assumptions, and inspect the holdings behind the numbers.

Long-term AI infrastructure investing is the core. Swing trading is a supplement. The reports explain what changed, what could change a decision, and which questions still need evidence. Proposals and confirmed trades stay separate.

The first model covers all recorded stocks and funds across three brokerage accounts, with no new deposits or withdrawals. Its probabilities are conditional estimates, with limited historical proxy validation. The page keeps its exclusions, sensitivity and validation visible beside the charts.

Older editions remain available, and future weekly reports appear in the archive automatically. Original forecasts keep their target dates and assumptions so they can be reviewed later.
