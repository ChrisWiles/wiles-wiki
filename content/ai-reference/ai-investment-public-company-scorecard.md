This is investment research, not financial advice. The page is a scorecard, not a recommendation list. Multiples, backlogs, and estimate-revision directions are pulled as of 2026-08-05 ± 1 month (Q2 2026 earnings cycle for most calendar-year reporters) and will move with prints, guidance updates, and tape action. The structural columns — bucket, bottleneck controlled, AI/DC revenue exposure, customer concentration — are more durable than the valuation columns and are the better lens for thesis durability. Cells marked `n/d` are not disclosed by the company at the level of granularity required; cells marked `n/a` are not a meaningful metric for that name (typical of pre-EBITDA neoclouds and miners with HPC pivots).

## How to read this scorecard

- **Bottleneck controlled** is the single most important column. A name with no scarce asset is just an AI narrative.
- **Estimate-revision direction (12m)** matters more than the absolute multiple. A 30x P/E with rising estimates compresses faster than a 15x P/E with falling estimates. `↑` = consensus 2026/2027 EPS revised up over the trailing 12 months; `→` = flat; `↓` = down.
- **Purity discounts the multiple needed**. A pure-play (Vertiv, Modine data center segment, neoclouds) commands a higher multiple before it is "expensive" than a diversified industrial with 10% data center exposure.
- **Backlog or book-to-bill** > 1.0 with sequential growth is the cleanest leading indicator the bottleneck thesis is working. Watch sequential change, not just absolute level.

## Highest-conviction by bucket

- **Electrical**: ETN and SU.PA — only platforms able to deliver complete prefab electrical systems at hyperscale; backlog growth confirms; Boyd Thermal acq adds liquid-cooling content per MW. POWL is the small-cap pure expression.
- **Power gen**: GEV — gas turbine slots are the binding constraint on time-to-power and backlog visibility now extends to 2027+. ENR.DE is the European pair-trade with comparable Grid Tech profile.
- **Memory**: MU and 000660.KS — HBM/DRAM essentially sold out 2026; structural under-supply continues into 2027. Multiple is still cyclical-anchored despite secular setup.
- **Semis/equip**: ASML (EUV monopoly + €38.8B backlog) and BESI (hybrid bonding pure-play). For broader exposure, NVDA remains the volume engine but multiple compression risk is highest if hyperscaler capex wobbles.
- **Optics/networking**: COHR (vertically integrated, datacom + indium phosphide) and ANET (only credible Ethernet alternative to Nvidia networking at scale).
- **Cooling**: VRT (the only liquid-cooling pure play with $12.45B backlog) and MOD (highest growth purity at smaller cap).
- **EPC**: PWR (utility T&D + DC scope) and EME (cleanest DC backlog disclosure with 32% RPO growth).
- **Neocloud / power-first**: NBIS (cleanest balance sheet; >$50B contracted backlog, MSFT+Meta anchors) and IREN (Microsoft-anchored GPU cloud deal, diversifying beyond BTC mining). CRWV is the largest but most leveraged, and its credit spreads have deteriorated sharply since April (see [[risk-tripwires-and-warning-indicators]] indicator #7) — the financing-risk canary for the whole bucket. WULF's new 20-yr, up-to-$33B Anthropic lease (announced 8/5/26) is the single largest contract event across the group this cycle and materially diversifies its counterparty base beyond Fluidstack/Core42.
- **Utilities/IPPs**: CEG and TLN — nuclear PPAs are the scarcest power product hyperscalers can buy; Vistra is the gas-and-nuclear pair. SO's new 3.2GW/25-yr OpenAI PPA is the first landmark large-load anchor from a traditional regulated Southeast utility.
- **Second-order**: FCX (copper deficit thesis, though Grasberg mud-rush cut FY26 guide), KMI/WMB (gas pipeline backlog tied to DC-driven gen).

## Estimate revision watch

The cleanest leading-indicator names where 12-month EPS revision direction is closely tied to whether the bottleneck is binding:

- **MU / 000660.KS** — Micron's shift to take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs) and SK hynix's LTA-based pricing make DRAM/HBM EPS far more mechanical and forward-visible than the historical spot-price cycle.
- **VRT** — FY26 EPS guide raised repeatedly to $6.65-6.75; consensus revisions keep chasing guidance.
- **GEV** — backlog hit 116GW and $176B total Q2'26, but 2027 consensus EPS shows unusually wide analyst dispersion (~$15 Street vs. $24+ bull case) — the next print either confirms or resets that spread.
- **ANET** — FY26 revenue guide raised to $12.6B, +$2.1B above the prior analyst-day target; next raise is the catalyst.
- **CORZ / WULF / HUT** — HPC contract-to-GAAP-revenue conversion (WULF's new Anthropic lease, HUT's River Bend/Beacon Point, CORZ's AMD deal) triggers the first real forward estimate sets as billing starts in 2H26/2027.
- **TLN / VST / CEG / SO** — additional hyperscaler PPAs (SO's new 3.2GW OpenAI deal, TLN's pending Cornerstone acquisition) are non-linear EBITDA events.
- **CRWV** — the inverse case: consensus EPS was cut ~19% in the 30 days before its Q2 print as light guidance and blown-out credit spreads (see tripwire #7) forced a re-rate; watch whether the Aug 11 print stabilizes or extends the cut.

The negative watch list (where revisions could go ↓ if hyperscaler capex pace softens or large-load tariffs proliferate): D (FY26 guide already cut below prior consensus on margin pressure), neoclouds/GPU lessors broadly given the neocloud-credit tripwire breach, AMAT (China headwind), MTZ (Communications segment miss dragged an otherwise-record backlog print).

## Caveats and disclosures

Multiples shown are NTM consensus where extractable, otherwise approximated from public commentary; they will move with every print and tape close. Backlogs are reported with a one-quarter lag and definitions vary (RPOs vs orders vs reservation agreements vs slot reservations are not directly comparable). 5-yr median multiples are eyeballed from public charts and labelled `n/m` for post-IPO or post-spin names where the history is too short. Customer concentration is rarely disclosed at top-1; many entries are inferred from SEC filings and conference commentary and labelled `^[ambiguous]`. Net leverage is approximated from the most recent 10-Q and excludes operating leases. Private-company peers (Schneider's private competitors in DCIM, private cooling specialists, private gas-turbine packagers, private contract miners) are out of scope. This is investment research, not a recommendation; do your own work.

**Data gaps this refresh (2026-08-05 cycle)**: MHI's dedicated research pass returned no usable data this cycle — its row was backfilled same-day from a single dated ($35.6B backlog, Sept-2025) secondary source and carries forward prior-cycle multiples largely unchanged; treat it as the weakest row on the page. SEI's Q2 2026 print released the same day as this refresh and is not reflected (row still shows Q1'26 figures). CEG, VST, and TLN report Q2 2026 results on 8/5-8/7/26, straddling this refresh date — their rows reflect the latest confirmed (Q1'26) prints, not the imminent Q2 releases. Several EV/EBITDA cells for early-ramp or negative-EBITDA names (GEV, BE, CRWV, and most neoclouds/power-first miners) are flagged `^[ambiguous]` or `n/a` because trailing multiples are distorted by a still-small EBITDA base — read backlog and revenue growth as the more reliable signal for those names this cycle.
