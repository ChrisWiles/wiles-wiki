**Recommended dates: Tue 13 Oct → Tue 20 Oct 2026 (7 nights).** Fly AUS ⇄ BOS nonstop, one rental car, three bases moving south down the coast.

This is the option where the scenery is *ocean plus color* rather than color alone. Acadia's granite domes drop straight into the Atlantic, and the hiking is short-but-vertical — iron rungs bolted into cliffs rather than long ridge slogs. The back half unwinds through Camden and Portland, which is one of the best food towns in the country.

## Why these dates

Coastal Maine peaks **Oct 10–20**, later than inland Maine and later than Vermont — Maine's peak now runs roughly two weeks later than it did in 1950. Mid-to-late October is also when Acadia's granite goes from crowded to quiet.

Starting **Tuesday Oct 13** deliberately skips **Indigenous Peoples' / Columbus Day weekend (Oct 10–12)**, which is Acadia's single busiest weekend. You arrive the day the crowds leave, into peak color.

**The trade-off:** a lot of Mount Desert Island shuts down right after that weekend. Assume the free **Island Explorer shuttle has stopped running** (it typically ends around Columbus Day), that some Bar Harbor restaurants and inns are closed for the season, and verify **Jordan Pond House** popover hours before building a day around them. You'll be driving and parking yourself all week — which is fine in late October, because the lots are no longer full.

## Flights

- **No AUS → Portland (PWM) nonstop.** Everything routes through a hub.
- Best plan: **AUS → BOS nonstop** (Delta / JetBlue / Southwest, ~4h05, ~100/week), then drive. The morning departure lands 12:28.
- Alternative: connect to **Bangor (BGR)**, which is only ~1h from Bar Harbor and saves ~3h of driving each way — worth pricing if the connection isn't brutal.
- Estimated fare: **$300–450 RT per person** via Boston.

## Car

Boston pickup, full-size. Estimate **$450–700 for the week**. This itinerary is ~600 miles of driving, most of it beautiful.

## The route

```
BOS ──2h00──▶ Portland, ME (1 night)
      ──3h00──▶ Bar Harbor (4 nights)
      ──2h00──▶ Camden (1 night)
      ──2h00──▶ Portland (1 night) ──2h00──▶ BOS
```

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Tue Oct 13 · Land and eat
Boston at 12:28, car, north to **Portland** by ~15:30. Walk the Old Port. Dinner: **Eventide Oyster Co.** (brown butter lobster roll — order it), **Duckfat** for the poutine, or **Fore Street** if you want the serious sit-down. Night 1 Portland.

### Day 2 — Wed Oct 14 · Up the coast to Mount Desert Island
Drive to Bar Harbor (3h). Afternoon in Acadia to get oriented:
- **Ocean Path** from Sand Beach to Otter Point (4.4 mi round trip, flat) past **Thunder Hole**
- **Great Head Trail** (1.7 mi) for the first real granite scramble
- Sunset at **Bass Harbor Head Light** on the island's southwest tip

Nights 2–5 in Bar Harbor.

### Day 3 — Thu Oct 15 · Cadillac sunrise, then the rungs
**Cadillac Summit Road requires a $6 vehicle reservation through Oct 25, 2026**, sold only on [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/). Two release waves: 30% at 90 days out (already gone for these dates), and **70% at 10:00 EST two days prior** — that's your shot, and it sells out in minutes. Set an alarm for 09:55 Central on Oct 13. Sunrise reservations get a 90-minute entry window; daytime, 30 minutes.

From roughly early October through early March, Cadillac is the first place in the United States to see sunrise. It's worth the 05:00 alarm.

Then the signature Acadia hikes — both are cliff climbs with iron rungs and ladders, not ordinary trails:
- **Beehive Loop** — 1.5 mi, 500 ft, exposed rungs above Sand Beach. Short, genuinely airy, not for anyone uneasy with heights.
- **Precipice Trail** — 2.5 mi, 1,000 ft, much more serious. Closed in spring for nesting peregrine falcons but normally **open by fall** — confirm at the visitor center the morning of.

Dinner in Bar Harbor.

### Day 4 — Fri Oct 16 · Jordan Pond and the twin summits
The best full day of hiking on the island: **Jordan Pond → Penobscot Mountain → Sargent Mountain loop**, roughly 6 mi with 1,500 ft of climbing, including the Jordan Cliffs if they're open. Sargent is the island's second-highest summit and far quieter than Cadillac.

Afternoon: **popovers at Jordan Pond House** (seasonal — verify), or the **carriage roads**. Rockefeller built 45 miles of crushed-stone carriage road with 17 hand-cut granite bridges, closed to cars. Rent bikes in Bar Harbor and ride the **Around-the-Mountain loop** (11 mi) — arguably the best way to experience Acadia's color.

### Day 5 — Sat Oct 17 · The quiet side
Cross to the western half of the island, which gets a fraction of the visitors:
- **Beech Mountain** (1.1 mi loop to a fire tower) or **Mansell / Bernard** for something longer
- **Ship Harbor** and **Wonderland** — easy shoreline loops
- Lunch in **Southwest Harbor** or **Bass Harbor** — this is where you eat lobster off a dock rather than off a menu
- **Asticou and Thuya Gardens** in Northeast Harbor if they're still open

### Day 6 — Sun Oct 18 · South to Camden
Drive to **Camden** (2h). This is where the trip changes gear. Camden Harbor, the schooners, and **Mount Battie** — you can hike it (1.1 mi, steep) or just drive the auto road to the top for the view over Penobscot Bay that ends up on every Maine calendar. **Maiden Cliff** (2 mi) is the better hike if legs are still willing.

**Farnsworth Art Museum** in Rockland for the Wyeth collection. Night 6 Camden.

### Day 7 — Mon Oct 19 · The long slow coast
Camden → Portland with stops: **Rockland Breakwater Lighthouse** (a mile-long granite jetty out into the bay), **Wiscasset** for Red's Eats if it hasn't closed for the season, **Freeport** if anyone wants L.L. Bean at midnight — it's open 24 hours and has been since 1951.

Night 7 Portland. Final dinner: **Central Provisions**, **Twelve**, or back to Eventide because you'll want to.

### Day 8 — Tue Oct 20 · Home
Morning at **Portland Head Light** in Cape Elizabeth (15 min from downtown, the most photographed lighthouse in America) and **The Holy Donut** for the road. Portland → BOS is 2h. Evening flight home.

## Lodging

**Bar Harbor (4 nights)** — [Bar Harbor Inn](https://www.barharborinn.com/) (harborfront, open latest into the season), [West Street Hotel](https://www.weststreethotel.com/) (rooftop pool, adults-only), [Atlantic Oceanside](https://www.barharbormainehotel.com/) for value. **Check closing dates** — a lot of MDI lodging shuts down between Oct 15 and Nov 1.

**Camden (1 night)** — [16 Bay View](https://16bayview.com/), [Whitehall](https://whitehallmaine.com/), or the [Norumbega Inn](https://www.norumbegainn.com/) if you want the stone-castle experience.

**Portland (2 nights)** — [The Press Hotel](https://www.thepresshotel.com/), [Longfellow Hotel](https://longfellowhotel.com/), or Canopy by Hilton in the Old Port.

Estimate **$250–500/night**. Unverified.

## Book now, in this order

1. **Bar Harbor lodging Oct 14–18**, confirming it's open that late in the season.
2. Flights and car.
3. Camden and Portland nights.
4. **Cadillac sunrise reservation at 10:00 EST on Oct 13** — the two-days-prior release. Have a backup plan; sunrise from Ocean Path is also excellent and needs nothing.

## Risks

- **Seasonal shutdown** is the big one. Verify every restaurant and inn; October 15 is a real cliff on Mount Desert Island.
- **Weather** — coastal Maine in late October is 55°F and windy on a good day, 45°F and raining sideways on a bad one. Rain gear, not just a fleece.
- **The rung trails** (Beehive, Precipice) are genuinely exposed and close when icy. Have Sargent/Penobscot ready as the substitute.
- Color here is beautiful but **less saturated than Vermont's interior** — the ocean is doing half the work. If the point of the trip is *maximum foliage*, [[vermont-white-mountains|Option A]] wins.
