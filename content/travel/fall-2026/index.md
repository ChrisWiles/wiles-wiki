Five fully planned one-week trips out of Austin, all built on the same brief: **hike hard for the first half, downshift for the second.** Three are eastern fall-color trips in October; two are western national-park trips that only work in September.

## The timing map

                            │   Oct 10–12       eastern week)

**The single highest-leverage decision is the week, not the place.** Every one of these destinations is mediocre two weeks off-peak and extraordinary in its window.

## My recommendation

**If you pick one: [[vermont-white-mountains|Option A, Vermont + White Mountains, Oct 2–9]].** It has the most reliable color of the five, the cleanest flights (AUS→BOS nonstop both ways), the best hard-then-easy structure — Franconia Ridge on Day 2, a spa in Stowe on Day 6 — and it needs almost no advance reservations beyond lodging. It also dodges Columbus Day weekend entirely.

**If you pick two: add [[glacier|Option E, Glacier, Sep 12–19]].** They don't compete — September Montana and October New England are different trips in different seasons, and the new Delta Saturday nonstop to Kalispell makes Glacier unusually easy to reach from Austin this year. Glacier is the better *hiking*; Vermont is the better *fall*.

**If budget matters most: [[asheville-blue-ridge|Option C, Asheville]]**, at roughly $500–1,000 less than the New England weeks for the same seven nights, with the most forgiving timing window because color is staggered by elevation.

## Calendar reality check

Your [[../china-2026/index|Asia trip is locked for Nov 13–29]]. Adding a September western trip *and* an October eastern trip means **three trips in ten weeks**, roughly five weeks away from home between mid-September and the end of November.

Two workable shapes:

- **One trip:** Vermont Oct 2–9. Six weeks of breathing room before Asia.
- **Two trips:** Glacier Sep 12–19 and Vermont Oct 2–9. Two clear weeks between each, and three between Vermont and the Asia departure.

Three is possible but stacks Asheville or Maine against a mid-October return and a Nov 13 long-haul.

## What to book first, regardless of choice

1. **Lodging.** Peak-week inventory in Stowe, Bar Harbor, Many Glacier, and Jackson is already thin in August, and the best properties go 6–12 months out.
2. **Rental cars.** Every one of these trips is car-dependent, and Kalispell and Bozeman supply is genuinely tight.
3. **Flights last** — they're the most flexible piece and the least likely to sell out.
4. **America the Beautiful pass, $80** — worth it the moment you visit two national parks.

## Verify before you commit

Everything here is researched but nothing is booked, and a few items are specifically flagged as needing confirmation:

- **Delta's Saturday-only AUS→BZN and AUS→FCA nonstops** launched June 13, 2026 and run "through the fall" — confirm the last operating Saturday before building a Montana week around them.
- **New England drought.** A warm, dry summer through August could mute color or drop leaves early. Re-check the foliage maps in mid-September and be willing to shift Option A four days earlier.
- **Blue Ridge Parkway construction** north of Asheville (MP 355–382) — Option C is routed south of it deliberately, but check current closures.
- **Acadia's seasonal shutdown** after Columbus Day, and the **Cadillac Summit Road $6 reservation** required through Oct 25.
- **Glacier's boats and shuttles** — all "through September, weather permitting."
- All prices are **estimates for planning, not verified rates.**
