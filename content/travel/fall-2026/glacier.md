**Recommended dates: Sat 12 Sep → Sat 19 Sep 2026 (7 nights).** Fly AUS ⇄ FCA nonstop, one rental car, three bases.

The most spectacular hiking of the five options, full stop. Glacier in September means Going-to-the-Sun Road without a reservation requirement, trails without summer crowds, and larch and aspen just beginning to turn gold against grey limestone.

## Why these dates

- **No vehicle reservations in 2026.** The park dropped the system entirely — a significant simplification versus recent years. New this year: **three-hour timed parking at Logan Pass** and a **ticketed Logan Pass Shuttle** for Highline Trail hikers, both in effect from July 1.
- **Going-to-the-Sun Road** opened fully June 22, 2026 and typically stays open to through-traffic until **mid-to-late October**, closing earlier if it snows hard.
- **Shuttles run July through September**, weather permitting — mid-September should still have service, but verify.
- **Larch and aspen:** first gold in valleys below 5,500 ft around **Sep 20–25**, with the real larch show late September into mid-October. Sep 12–19 catches the very start of it.
- **Weather:** West Glacier / Lake McDonald (3,200 ft) runs 60–65°F daytime; Logan Pass (6,646 ft) is 10–15°F colder. Snow at lower elevations is possible from mid-September.

### The date trade-off, stated plainly

| Week | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep 12–19 (recommended)** | Everything still open, shuttles running, best hiking weather, low snow risk | Color barely starting |
| Sep 19–26 | Larch and aspen actually turning, emptiest trails | Many Glacier Hotel and other in-park lodging closing, higher snow risk on Logan Pass |

If the color itself matters more than the hiking, take the later week and base outside the park in Whitefish, which is open year-round. **If Glacier is the hiking trip and [[vermont-white-mountains|Vermont]] or [[asheville-blue-ridge|Asheville]] is the foliage trip, take Sep 12–19 and don't compromise the hiking.**

## Flights

**Delta launched AUS → FCA (Glacier Park International, Kalispell) nonstop on June 13, 2026 — Saturdays only**, SkyWest as Delta Connection, ~3h55, seasonal through the fall. Saturday-only service is exactly why this is a Sat-to-Sat week.

- **Verify the last operating Saturday before booking.** Fallback is a connection through SLC or MSP.
- Estimated fare: **$350–550 RT per person**.
- FCA is 30 min from Whitefish and 40 min from West Glacier — the most convenient park airport in the system.

## Car

Full-size SUV. Estimate **$600–850 for the week** — Kalispell rental supply is limited and expensive, so book this early, earlier than the hotels. Going-to-the-Sun Road has a **21-foot length and 8-foot width limit** between Avalanche Creek and Rising Sun; a standard SUV is fine, an oversized truck is not.

## The route

```
FCA ──40m──▶ West Glacier / Apgar (3 nights)   [west side, hike hard]
      ──2h00──▶ St. Mary or Many Glacier (2 nights)  [east side, hike hard]
      ──2h30──▶ Whitefish (2 nights)           [relax]
      ──30m──▶ FCA
```

Going over the Sun Road as a *transfer* rather than a day trip is the trick here — you drive the best road in America with your luggage in the back and never repeat it.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Sat Sep 12 · Land and Lake McDonald
FCA at midday, car, into the park by mid-afternoon. Easy first day at altitude:
- **Lake McDonald** and the famous colored stones on the shore at Apgar
- **Trail of the Cedars / Avalanche Lake** (4.5 mi round trip, 750 ft) — an old-growth cedar boardwalk that opens onto a lake ringed with waterfalls. The best short hike in the park.
- Sunset from the Apgar dock, looking straight up the lake

Nights 1–3 in West Glacier / Apgar.

### Day 2 — Sun Sep 13 · Highline Trail
**The signature hike.** Logan Pass → Highline Trail, following the Continental Divide with a 100-mile view and a section of ledge with a hand cable bolted into the cliff. Options:
- **Haystack Butte and back** — 7.6 mi, moderate
- **Granite Park Chalet and back** — 15.2 mi, or 11.8 mi one-way descending **The Loop** and shuttling back
- Add the **Grinnell Glacier Overlook** spur (1.6 mi, 950 ft, brutally steep) for the single best view in the park

Logan Pass parking fills by 07:30 even in September, and there's now a **three-hour parking limit** — which means for a full Highline day you want the **ticketed Logan Pass Shuttle**. Sort this out in advance; it's the one genuine logistics puzzle of the trip.

### Day 3 — Mon Sep 14 · Two Medicine or the west-side quiet
Either:
- **Two Medicine** (2h drive around the south end) — **Scenic Point** (7.4 mi, 2,300 ft) or the boat across Two Medicine Lake to **Upper Two Medicine Lake**. This corner sees a fraction of the traffic and has the best aspen stands.
- Or stay west: **Rocky Point** and **Apgar Lookout** (7.1 mi, 1,800 ft) — good larch, big Lake McDonald views, and a low-effort day if legs need one.

### Day 4 — Tue Sep 15 · Over the Sun Road to the east side
The transfer day, and one of the great drives on earth. **Going-to-the-Sun Road** west to east: Lake McDonald → The Loop → Bird Woman Falls → the Weeping Wall → Logan Pass → Jackson Glacier Overlook → St. Mary Lake and **Wild Goose Island**.

Stop and walk the **Hidden Lake Overlook** (2.7 mi from Logan Pass, boardwalk then trail, mountain goats basically guaranteed) and **St. Mary and Virginia Falls** (3.6 mi) on the way down.

Nights 4–5 at **Many Glacier** or **St. Mary**. Good news for 2026: the Swiftcurrent construction that disrupted Many Glacier in 2025 is **complete, with no road construction anticipated in the area this year**.

### Day 5 — Wed Sep 16 · Grinnell Glacier
**The best day hike in the park.** 10.6 mi and 2,200 ft from the Many Glacier Hotel, or **7.6 mi** if you take the two boats across Swiftcurrent and Josephine Lakes — verify the boats are still running in mid-September, they're an early casualty of the shoulder season.

You climb past three lakes, each a more impossible shade of turquoise than the last, to a glacier and an iceberg-dotted tarn. Bear activity in this valley is high; carry spray, make noise, and check trail status at the ranger station that morning.

Shorter alternatives in the same valley, all excellent for aspen color: **Redrock Falls** (3.6 mi), **Bullhead Lake** (6.8 mi), **Apikuni Falls** (2 mi).

### Day 6 — Thu Sep 17 · East side, then west to Whitefish
Morning: **Iceberg Lake** (9.7 mi, 1,200 ft — surprisingly gentle for the payoff) if there's another big day in you, or the short **Swiftcurrent Lake loop** and a slow breakfast on the Many Glacier Hotel deck.

Afternoon: drive back over the Sun Road, or around via US-2 and **Marias Pass** for the different view. Into **Whitefish** by evening. Nights 6–7.

### Day 7 — Fri Sep 18 · Whitefish, deliberately unproductive
The relax day. **Whitefish Lake** — rent a paddleboard if it's warm, or just sit on the beach at City Beach. **Downtown Whitefish** is a real town with good restaurants (Latitude 48, Tupelo Grille, Loula's for breakfast) rather than a park gateway strip.

Add if wanted: the **Whitefish Mountain Resort** gondola for a summit view without walking, a spa afternoon at **The Firebrand** or Grouse Mountain Lodge, or the **Hungry Horse Reservoir** loop drive for larch.

### Day 8 — Sat Sep 19 · Home
FCA is 30 minutes from Whitefish. Easiest departure of any of the five options.

## Lodging

**West Glacier / Apgar (3 nights)** — [Lake McDonald Lodge](https://www.glaciernationalparklodges.com/) (in-park, historic, books ~13 months out), [Belton Chalet](https://beltonchalet.com/) at the west entrance, or the Cedar Creek / Under Canvas options just outside.

**Many Glacier / St. Mary (2 nights)** — [Many Glacier Hotel](https://www.glaciernationalparklodges.com/) is the one to want: a Swiss-style lodge on Swiftcurrent Lake with the best hotel view in the National Park system. It books out nearly a year ahead and **closes for the season in late September** — confirm your dates fall inside. [St. Mary Village](https://www.glacierparkcollection.com/) is the reliable fallback.

**Whitefish (2 nights)** — [The Firebrand](https://firebrandhotel.com/), Grouse Mountain Lodge, or Lodge at Whitefish Lake.

Estimate **$250–450/night**, with in-park historic lodges at the top of that.

## Book now, in this order

1. **Confirm the Delta AUS→FCA Saturday nonstop operates Sep 12 and Sep 19.**
2. **Many Glacier Hotel** — check availability immediately and confirm the season-closing date. This single booking is worth reshaping the week around.
3. Rental SUV out of FCA (tighter supply than the hotels).
4. Logan Pass Shuttle tickets for the Highline day, once the booking process is published.
5. West Glacier and Whitefish nights.

## Risks

- **Snow on Logan Pass.** A September storm can close Going-to-the-Sun Road for a day or two, which would strand this itinerary's transfer plan. The workaround is US-2 around the south end (2h30 instead of 2h). Build a flex day into your thinking.
- **Seasonal shutdown of boats and shuttles** — the Grinnell and Two Medicine boats and the Logan Pass shuttle are all "through September, weather permitting." Losing them adds miles to several hikes. Verify in early September.
- **Bears.** Grinnell and the Many Glacier valley in general have high grizzly activity, and September is hyperphagia season when bears are feeding hard before denning. Bear spray on the belt, not in the pack.
- **This is the most physically demanding of the five.** Grinnell, Highline, and Iceberg back-to-back is a real week. The Whitefish finish exists for a reason.
