Four nights, Nov 25–29. Taipei is the mainland exit, the Thanksgiving stop and the recovery finish.

## Where to stay

| Hotel | Area | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Kimpton Da An** | Da’an | First choice: design, MRT, restaurants and neighborhood energy |
| Regent Taipei | Zhongshan | Conventional luxury near dining and Ningxia Night Market |
| Capella Taipei | Songshan | High-design 2025 splurge; choose only if the hotel is part of the experience |

## Daily plan

### Wed Nov 25 — arrive + Raohe

Prefer SHA → TSA for city-center convenience if the schedule and fare work. Check in, keep dinner casual and graze at Raohe Night Market: Fuzhou black-pepper bun, herbal pork-rib soup, oyster vermicelli and mochi.

### Thu Nov 26 — Thanksgiving

Dihua Street and Longshan Temple in the morning. Leave the afternoon open for **Thanksgiving dinner with Jen’s family**.

### Fri Nov 27 — museum + skyline

Focused half-day at the National Palace Museum. Finish with Elephant Mountain before sunset and Taipei 101/Xinyi after dark.

### Sat Nov 28 — choose one finale

- **Beitou** for hot springs and recovery, or
- **Jiufen/Shifen** for a longer atmospheric day trip.

Do not attempt both.

### Sun Nov 29 — fly home

TPE → AUS on the protected open-jaw ticket.

## Food targets

- Mountain and Sea House — refined Taiwanese heritage meal; reserve.
- Din Tai Fung — choose a convenient branch rather than waiting at the original.
- Raohe Night Market — compact arrival-night crawl.
- Jin Feng — braised pork rice.
- Soy milk + youtiao breakfast and one beef-noodle lunch.
