Five nights, Nov 12–17. Hong Kong is the jet-lag landing base and contains the optional [[shenzhen|Shenzhen day trip]].

| Hotel | Area | Call |
|---|---|---|

| Eaton HK | Jordan | Group-value option near Kowloon food and West Kowloon |

## Daily plan

### Thu Nov 12 — arrive

Airport Express, check in and eat close to the hotel. Do not attach timed plans to the long-haul arrival.

### Fri Nov 13 — Central + Peak

Tai Kwun, the Mid-Levels escalator, Man Mo Temple and the Central–Sheung Wan lanes. Ride the “ding ding” tram, then take bus 15 up if the Peak Tram line is ugly. Walk Lugard Road through blue hour and take the tram down.

### Sat Nov 14 — Shenzhen option

Attempt the Shenzhen port visa at Luohu or Huanggang. If issued, run the compact Futian loop and return that evening. If refused, use the day for Lamma, Sham Shui Po or a slow Hong Kong neighborhood day.

### Sun Nov 15 — West Kowloon

M+, waterfront, Tsim Sha Tsui and the Star Ferry around sunset.

### Mon Nov 16 — outdoors

Dragon’s Back to Big Wave Bay: roughly 8 km / four hours. Wet-weather fallback: Tai Kwun, Hong Kong Palace Museum, shopping or spa.

### Tue Nov 17 — Chongqing

Direct HKG → CKG. Keep the confirmed Shanghai → Taipei ticket accessible for the 240-hour temporary-entry desk.

## Food

- The Chairman — hardest reservation; plan around it only if the official release succeeds.
- Yardbird — whole-bird yakitori and whisky; lively group dinner.
- Yat Lok — quarter roast goose with lai fun.
- Mak’s Noodle / Kau Kee — quick classic lunches.
- Temple Street or a dai pai dong — flexible casual evening.

## Practical

- Octopus and contactless cards cover transit and most small spending.
- Admiralty connects directly to East Rail for the Luohu outing.
- Peak Tram and Dragon’s Back should remain weather-sensitive.
- Official ideas: [Hong Kong Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/attractions/your-guide-to-experiencing-hong-kong.html).

## Traveler consensus

Cross-checking current travel writing, TripAdvisor reviews and Hong Kong local guidance supports four protected experiences: the Peak/Lugard Road, Central–Sheung Wan on foot, the Star Ferry at dusk and Dragon’s Back in clear weather. M+ is an excellent culture/rain anchor, but it depends on the group’s interest.

- The view—not a long Peak Tram queue or paid Sky Terrace—is the essential part. Bus 15 up and tram down is the best crowd fallback.
- Avenue of Stars is a pleasant harborfront connector, not a half-day attraction.
- Skip the Observation Wheel and Sky100 unless the group specifically wants another paid skyline view.
- Sources: [Star Ferry traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294217-d2228870-Reviews-Star_Ferry-Hong_Kong.html) and [Time Out’s local reality check](https://www.timeout.com/hong-kong/things-to-do/11-tourist-attractions-hongkongers-never-go-to).

## Expanded catalog

These are worthwhile additions, not extra obligations:

- **M+ Museum** — strongest rainy-day half-day; allow 2–3 hours and pair it with West Kowloon. [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294217-d23742759-Reviews-M_Museum-Hong_Kong.html)
- **Chi Lin Nunnery + Nan Lian Garden** — calm temple-and-garden contrast, best near opening. [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g294217-d437839-Reviews-Chi_Lin_Nunnery-Hong_Kong.html)
- **Sham Shui Po** — working markets, street texture and casual food; build a compact circuit rather than chasing isolated pins.
- **Sai Kung** — optional clear-weather half/full day when coastal scenery matters more than another urban district.
- **Ho Lee Fook** — lively modern-Cantonese substitute when The Chairman is unavailable. [Traveler reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g294217-d6758068-Reviews-Ho_Lee_Fook-Hong_Kong.html)
- **Australia Dairy Company** — brisk cha-chaan-teng breakfast; skip if the group dislikes queues or rushed service.
- **One Dim Sum** — useful value lunch near Prince Edward, but compare the live wait.
