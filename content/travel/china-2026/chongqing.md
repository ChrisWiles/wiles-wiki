The hotpot capital and the most visually surreal big city in China — a mountain city stacked in layers over the Yangtze/Jialing confluence, where the metro runs *through* an apartment block and "ground floor" is a matter of opinion. Route G gives it the food-city slot that Route A gives Guangzhou.

<img src="_attachments/china-2026/chongqing/hongya-cave-night.jpg" alt="Hongya Cave stilt houses lit gold at night" />

## Why it's on the list

- **Food.** Chongqing hotpot is the spiciest, most social meal in China — a nine-grid (九宫格) cauldron of bubbling beef tallow. Plus xiaomian noodle culture, maoxuewang, and Wanzhou grilled fish. For a foodie friends trip this is the single highest-density eating city in the lineup.
- **The nightscape.** Hongya Cave's eleven stories of golden stilt-houses, the two-river skyline from Nanshan, and the Yangtze cableway make it the most photogenic urban stop of any route.
- **TWOV-clean.** Jiangbei (CKG) is a confirmed 240-hour port; Cathay flies HKG → CKG daily (~2.5 hr).

## Where to stay

| Area | Why | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Jiefangbei (CBD) | Walk to Hongya Cave, Raffles, riverfront; most convenient | $80–150 mid-range |
| Hongya Cave-adjacent river-view rooms | The view *is* the amenity | $120–200 |
| Nan'an / Nanbin Road | Across the river — skyline panorama, quieter | $70–130 |

Mid-range in Chongqing runs noticeably cheaper than Shanghai/HK — a small budget breather in the middle of the trip.

## The 3-day shape

**Day 1 (arrival, Tue).** Fly HKG → CKG (~2.5 hr, mainland entry, TWOV clock starts next midnight). Metro or DiDi to Jiefangbei. Evening: **Hongya Cave at night** — view first from Qiansimen Bridge, then wander in. First hotpot dinner.

**Day 2.** Morning **xiaomian** breakfast. **Yangtze River cableway** across to Nan'an (book the time slot in Alipay). **Shibati / Xiahao old streets**, then **Raffles Crystal** skybridge at sunset. Optional: Kuixinglou "8D city" viewpoints. Late: Jiufangge or another nine-grid hotpot.

**Day 3.** Either **Ciqikou ancient town** (porcelain-village alleys, suan la fen stalls — go before 10am, it swamps) plus **Eling Park + Liziba metro-through-building**, or a full **Wulong karst day trip** (Three Natural Bridges, *Transformers 4* location, ~2 hr each way by train/tour). Foodies stay in town; landscape people go to Wulong.

**Day 4 (departure, Fri).** Fly CKG → HGH (~2 hr) + car to Wuzhen (~75–90 min). Continue Route G.

## Eating (the point of the stop)

| Spot | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Zhao Er Hotpot (赵二火锅)** | The Lonely-Planet-blessed local institution | Stronger/spicier broth; duck blood, fresh tripe, cilantro meatballs. Moved in 2025 — check Amap. |
| Any packed nine-grid place | The real experience | Order 微辣 (mild). Chongqing mild ≈ everyone else's hot. Numbing ≠ broken tongue: drink soy milk, not water. |
| Xiaomian stalls near Jiefangbei | Breakfast | CNY 10–15. Follow office-worker queues. |
| Ciqikou snack alley | Suan la fen, chenmapo tofu pudding | Day 3 grazing |
| Nanbin Road riverside | Wanzhou grilled fish + beer | Late-night, skyline view |

## Practical

- **Getting around**: Metro is excellent and scenic (Line 2 along the river is half the sightseeing). DiDi cheap. Walking is *vertical* — expect stairs everywhere; comfortable shoes.
- **Weather**: mid-Oct 15–22°C and often misty; early Nov 12–18°C. The fog is atmospheric, not a problem — this is the "fog capital" year-round.
- **TWOV**: CKG Jiangbei confirmed 240-hr port ([chinaairlinetravel](https://www.chinaairlinetravel.com/airport-guide/chongqing-jiangbei/240-hour-visa-free-transit.html)). Chongqing municipality is one of the 24 eligible regions; onward internal flight to Zhejiang (Hangzhou) is fine.
- **Booking locks**: river-view Hongya-adjacent rooms 3–4 weeks ahead; Wulong day tour 3–5 days ahead; hotpot mostly walk-in (queue at peak — put your name in via WeChat mini-program where offered).
