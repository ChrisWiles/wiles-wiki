The postcard-China landscape: limestone karst towers over the Li River, bamboo rafts, rice paddies, and water buffalo. Route F's anchor — the landscape-drama alternative to [[zhangjiajie|Zhangjiajie]] that needs **zero flights** (Guangzhou South → Yangshuo is a 2.5-hr HSR hop).

<img src="_attachments/china-2026/guilin/li-river-karst.jpg" alt="Karst peaks over the Li River between Guilin and Yangshuo" />

## Why it's on the list

- **The Li River between Xingping and Yangshuo** is the scene on the CNY 20 note — mist, peaks, cormorant fishermen. Gentler than Zhangjiajie: you float through this landscape rather than climb it.
- **Yangshuo countryside** — cycle the Yulong River valley between paddies and peaks; bamboo-raft the Yulong; moon hill sunset.
- **All-rail access.** Guangxi is fully TWOV-eligible, and the whole stop chains on HSR: Guangzhou → Yangshuo → Guilin → Shenzhen.

## Base strategy: 3 nights Yangshuo + 1 night Guilin

Yangshuo town itself is loud and neon at the center — stay **outside town in the Yulong River valley** where the karst scenery is the front yard.

| Area | Why | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Yulong River valley guesthouses (Jiuxian / Chaolong villages) | Peaks-and-paddies setting, bike-out-the-door | $60–150 |
| Yangshuo West Street edge | Walkable to restaurants, still noisy | $50–100 |
| Guilin city (1 night) | Stage the Longji day or the Shenzhen HSR | $50–120 |

## The 4-day shape

**Day 1 (Wed).** HSR Guangzhou South → Yangshuo (~2.5 hr). Note: **Yangshuo station is ~40 min from town** near Xingping — pre-book the guesthouse car. Settle in, easy Yulong valley walk, beer fish dinner.

**Day 2.** **Li River day**: car to Xingping (~40 min), boat or hike the Xingping → Nine Horses Mural stretch, find the CNY-20-note viewpoint. Back via Yangshuo TV Tower or Cuiping Hill for sunset over the peaks.

**Day 3.** **Cycling + rafting day**: bike the Yulong River loop (flat, ~3–4 hrs with stops), bamboo raft a Yulong section (drifting, not whitewater), Moon Hill if legs allow. Massage + West Street evening.

**Day 4 (Sat).** Either transfer to **Guilin** (~1 hr) for Elephant Trunk Hill + a Guilin mifen crawl and an easy night before the train — or make it a **Longji rice terraces** long day (~2.5 hr each way; terraces are golden-harvest spectacular in late Sep–Oct, cut by early Nov) and sleep in Guilin.

**Day 5 (Sun).** HSR Guilin → Shenzhen North (~3.5 hr). Continue Route F.

## Eating

| Spot | What |
|---|---|
| Beer fish (啤酒鱼) along Xianqian Jie, Yangshuo | The signature — river fish braised in local beer. Pick a busy one; weigh-and-price before cooking. |
| Guilin mifen (桂林米粉) shops, Guilin | CNY 8 rice-noodle breakfast, pickles + peanuts. The city's daily ritual. |
| Yulong valley farmhouse restaurants | Bamboo-tube rice, stuffed Li River snails (酿田螺), stir-fried pumpkin shoots |
| West Street cafés | Yangshuo has had backpacker-coffee culture since the '80s — good flat whites with a karst view |

## Practical

- **Weather**: mid-Oct 17–26°C; early Nov 13–21°C, drier. Morning river mist is a feature. Karst photography is best in the soft light after rain.
- **TWOV**: Guangxi (Guilin, Yangshuo, Nanning + 9 more cities) is in the 24 eligible regions — cross-provincial HSR from Guangdong is explicitly allowed.
- **Crowds**: the Xingping boat piers swamp with tour groups 10:00–15:00 — go first boat or late afternoon. Yulong valley stays quiet.
- **Booking locks**: Yulong valley guesthouses 4–6 weeks ahead (small, they fill); Li River boat tickets 1–3 days ahead via Trip.com/hotel; Longji only needs a day-of car arrangement.
