Five nights, Nov 20–25. Shanghai is the longest mainland base and the exit point for Taipei.

## Where to stay

| Hotel | Area | Call |
|---|---|---|
| **The Langham** | Xintiandi | First choice: metro, walkable lanes and dining |
| Andaz Xintiandi | Xintiandi | Design alternative in the same strong base |
| SSAW Boutique | Old City / Bund edge | Rational value option |

## Daily plan

### Fri Nov 20 — arrive + Bund

Prefer CKG → SHA Hongqiao. Check into Xintiandi, rest, then walk the Bund after dark. Keep dinner reservation-free.

### Sat Nov 21 — Former French Concession

Wukang Mansion, Wukang/Anfu/Fuxing roads, cafés and boutiques. Reserve Fu 1088 for the local splurge.

### Sun Nov 22 — Museum East + Pudong

Shanghai Museum East, Lujiazui skyline and ferry back to Puxi at dusk. The museum’s 2026 schedule includes major cross-year exhibitions in this window.

### Mon Nov 23 — slow option

Choose a Zhujiajiao half-day for water-town atmosphere or stay in the city for West Bund museums and river walking. Monday is intentionally not built around traditional museum schedules.

### Tue Nov 24 — Old Shanghai

Yu Garden at opening, City God Temple lanes, xiao long bao and a final Bund or rooftop drink.

### Wed Nov 25 — Taipei

Fly SHA/PVG → TSA/TPE. Prefer SHA → TSA for convenience; use PVG → TPE if the schedule is substantially better. This flight ends the mainland admission.

## Food

- Fu 1088 — river shrimp, smoked mackerel belly and cashew-praline puff.
- Jia Jia Tang Bao — 127 Huanghe Road; pork and seasonal crab-roe XLB.
- Lai Lai Xiao Long — 506 Tianjin Road.
- Ren He Guan / Old Jesse — Shanghainese comfort food.
- Mr & Mrs Bund — one skyline-centered international dinner if desired.

## Practical

- Late November commonly runs 14–19°C by day and 8–13°C at night; pack for cold rain.
- Use Amap/Apple Maps and DiDi inside Alipay.
- Keep the Shanghai → Taipei confirmation printed and offline.
