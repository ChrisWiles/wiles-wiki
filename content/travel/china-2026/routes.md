## TWOV clock

The main transit is **Hong Kong → mainland China → Taiwan**:

- Enter at Chongqing Jiangbei on Tue Nov 17.
- The 240-hour period begins Wed Nov 18 at 00:00.
- Fly Shanghai → Taipei on Wed Nov 25.
- Ordinary latest exit is Fri Nov 27 at 23:59.
- Planned exit has roughly two days of buffer.

Carry a passport valid for at least three months, the confirmed Shanghai → Taipei ticket dated Nov 25, hotel reservations, and the temporary-entry arrival card. Admission remains subject to border inspection.

Official policy: [China National Immigration Administration](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c187308/content.html).

## Shenzhen is separate

The Shenzhen SEZ port visa and the later 240-hour transit are two different mainland admissions:

1. Hong Kong → Shenzhen → Hong Kong, same day.
2. Hong Kong → Chongqing → Shanghai → Taipei.

The first entry ends when mainland exit immigration is cleared on the return to Hong Kong. Keep any exit stamp, Hong Kong landing slip and transport receipts. Do not attempt to fly Shenzhen → Chongqing under the Shenzhen-only visa.
