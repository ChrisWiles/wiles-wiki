## Nov 17 — Hong Kong → Chongqing

Book a direct HKG → CKG flight. Allow the full airport day even though flight time is roughly 2½–3 hours. Chongqing Jiangbei is the mainland entry port for the 240-hour admission.

## Nov 20 — Chongqing → Shanghai

Book nonstop CKG → SHA or PVG. Prefer Hongqiao (SHA) when the timing and fare are close because it is substantially nearer the central hotel base.

## Nov 25 — Shanghai → Taipei

Best door-to-door combination: SHA → TSA. Best schedule depth: PVG → TPE. Either is a valid mainland exit; the confirmed ticket must be dated inside the 240-hour period.

## Shenzhen day trip

Use Luohu or Huanggang for the VOA request. The actual train/MTR leg may be short, but VOA processing, mainland immigration, local transit and the return border make it a full outing. Do not use West Kowloon expecting a Shenzhen port visa.

## Within cities

- Hong Kong: MTR, East Rail, Star Ferry and Octopus.
- Shenzhen: metro in Futian; keep the loop compact.
- Chongqing: metro/monorail plus DiDi. Elevation makes walking estimates deceptive.
- Shanghai: metro plus DiDi; Xintiandi is the most balanced base.
- Taipei: MRT, EasyCard and occasional taxi. TSA is the convenient regional airport.
