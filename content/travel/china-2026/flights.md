Book the trip in two layers: one protected **open-jaw long-haul** ticket and three regional one-ways.

## Long haul

### Austin → Hong Kong — arrive Thu Nov 12

No nonstop exists. Search departures on Nov 10 and Nov 11 to be in Hong Kong Nov 12. Favor a one-stop protected ticket through SFO, LAX, DFW, IAH or TPE. Optimize the long-haul cabin and connection quality before chasing the last small fare difference.

### Taipei → Austin — Sun Nov 29

Book on the same open-jaw ticket as the outbound. Favor one stop through SFO, LAX, SEA, DFW or IAH. Avoid self-transfers after a four-city itinerary.

Economy planning band for the open jaw: roughly **$900–1,600 per person** before bags and seat selection. Reprice live.

## Regional flights

| Date | Route | Booking call | Planning band |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Nov 17 | HKG → CKG | Nonstop only; late morning preferred | $120–250 |
| Nov 20 | CKG → SHA/PVG | Nonstop; prefer Hongqiao for city access | $80–180 |
| Nov 25 | SHA/PVG → TSA/TPE | Prefer city-center SHA → TSA; PVG → TPE has more depth | $150–300 |

At Chongqing immigration, the Nov 25 Shanghai → Taipei ticket is the required confirmed onward arrangement. Do not book HKG → CKG with an intermediate mainland stop: immigration would occur at that first mainland airport.

## Airport logic

- **HKG**: Airport Express is the easy arrival.
- **CKG**: Jiangbei is an approved 240-hour entry port.
- **SHA vs PVG**: Hongqiao usually saves substantial ground time from central Shanghai.
- **TSA vs TPE**: Songshan is the convenience win; Taoyuan carries more flights.
