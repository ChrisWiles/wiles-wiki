## Two separate mainland admissions

### Optional Shenzhen day trip

Hong Kong → Shenzhen under a Shenzhen SEZ port visa → Hong Kong the same day. Apply at Luohu or Huanggang; West Kowloon does not issue this visa. Stay inside Shenzhen municipality. On return, clear mainland exit immigration and retain the exit evidence and Hong Kong landing slip.

This outing is optional and cannot be allowed to threaten the main route. If the port visa is refused, continue the Hong Kong day.

### Main 240-hour transit

Hong Kong → Chongqing → Shanghai → Taipei.

- Enter CKG Nov 17.
- Clock starts Nov 18 at 00:00.
- Exit Shanghai for Taipei Nov 25.
- Ordinary deadline Nov 27 at 23:59.
- Carry the confirmed Nov 25 Shanghai → Taipei ticket.

Do not book HKG → CKG through another mainland airport. Do not route Shanghai → Taipei through Hong Kong.

Official policy: [China National Immigration Administration](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c187308/content.html). Reconfirm the exact route with NIA 12367 before travel; admission remains subject to inspection.

## Arrival card and registration

Complete the mainland arrival information before the CKG flight using NIA’s official arrival-card channel. Normal hotels register foreign guests; private accommodation may require local registration within 24 hours.

## Payments

### Mainland

- Configure Alipay and WeChat Pay at home with passport verification and linked cards.
- Carry more than one physical card and some RMB backup.
- Use Amap/Apple Maps rather than relying on Google Maps.

### Hong Kong

- Octopus plus contactless cards cover most daily spending.

### Taiwan

- Cards are common, but night markets and small shops still reward carrying TWD cash.
- EasyCard handles MRT and convenience-store purchases.

## Connectivity

Use a regional roaming eSIM that explicitly covers Hong Kong, mainland China and Taiwan, or separate mainland/Taiwan plans. Install and test any VPN/connectivity fallbacks before leaving the United States. Keep airline, hotel, ticket and border documents available offline.

## Document packet

- Original passports
- Hotel list in all four bases
- HKG → CKG boarding confirmation
- Shanghai → Taipei confirmed ticket
- Travel-insurance details
- Emergency card numbers
- Shenzhen VOA backup materials if attempting the outing

## Fallbacks

- Shenzhen VOA refusal: remain in Hong Kong.
- Weather cancels Dragon’s Back: M+, Tai Kwun or spa day.
- Shanghai → Taipei schedule change: rebook inside the 240-hour deadline before flying to China.
- Any uncertainty at CKG: show the complete HKG → CKG → Shanghai → Taipei chain and request the 240-hour temporary-entry desk.
