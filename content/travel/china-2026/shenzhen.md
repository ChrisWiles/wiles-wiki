Shenzhen is **not a hotel base** on the final route. It is a conditional same-day outing from Hong Kong, tentatively Sat Nov 14, only if the five-day Shenzhen Special Economic Zone port visa is issued.

## Border plan

- Use **Luohu or Huanggang**, where the port visa can be requested.
- Do not go to West Kowloon expecting a Shenzhen VOA; it does not issue one.
- Carry passport photos if possible, payment alternatives, Hong Kong accommodation details and the main-trip flight confirmations.
- If refused, return to the Hong Kong plan. No other reservation depends on Shenzhen.
- On the way back, clear mainland exit immigration and Hong Kong entry. Keep any passport stamps, landing slip and transport receipt.

The port visa permits travel inside Shenzhen municipality only. Do not fly from Shenzhen to Chongqing under it. The later HKG → CKG arrival is a separate mainland entry under the 240-hour policy.

## One-day Futian / Huaqiangbei loop

Keep the day geographically coherent:

1. Huaqiangbei and SEG Plaza for the hardware/electronics ecosystem.
2. Shenzhen Museum’s reform-era galleries for the city’s transformation story.
3. Lunch in the Civic Center / UpperHills area.
4. Lianhua Hill Park for the skyline; add Ping An’s paid deck only in exceptional visibility.
5. Return to Hong Kong that evening.

Skip distant Shekou, Window of the World, Splendid China and OCT-LOFT on this version. The short rail ride does not include VOA queues, two immigration processes and local metro time; treat this as a full outing. Cross-source one-day guidance consistently favors a compact look at the modern city over a replica theme park or cross-city mall crawl.
