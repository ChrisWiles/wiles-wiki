Sixteen hotel nights, Nov 13–29, from subtropical Hong Kong to damp/cool Chongqing and Shanghai, then mild Taipei.

## Expected conditions

| Stop | Typical day | Typical night | Character |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Hong Kong / Shenzhen | 24–27°C | 18–22°C | Usually drier; warm hiking weather |
| Chongqing | 14–18°C | 10–13°C | Misty and damp |
| Shanghai | 14–19°C | 8–13°C | Changeable; cold rain possible |
| Taipei | 21–25°C | 17–20°C | Mild, with northeast-monsoon showers possible |

## Capsule

- 4 breathable base-layer shirts
- 2 long-sleeve shirts
- 1 sweater or fleece
- 1 packable puffer or insulated jacket
- 1 genuinely waterproof light shell
- 2 everyday pants + 1 hiking/travel pant
- 1 pair trail-capable walking shoes + 1 cleaner dinner sneaker
- 7 sets underwear/socks; plan laundry at Ascott or mid-trip in Shanghai
- Compact umbrella, sun cap, sunglasses and small daypack
- Swimsuit for hotel pool or Beitou spa planning

## Documents

- Passports with at least six months validity preferred
- Printed Hong Kong, Chongqing, Shanghai and Taipei hotel confirmations
- Printed HKG → CKG and Shanghai → Taipei tickets
- Copies of the NIA 240-hour policy summary and emergency contacts
- Passport photos and payment backup for the optional Shenzhen VOA
- Travel-insurance policy

## Electronics and apps

- Universal adapter: Hong Kong Type G; mainland/Taiwan commonly use Type A plus local variants
- Power bank under airline limits
- Regional eSIM covering Hong Kong, mainland China and Taiwan, or separate plans
- Alipay + WeChat Pay, Octopus, Amap, Pleco and offline translation
- Two connectivity fallbacks configured before entering mainland China

## Health

- Prescriptions plus several spare days
- Ibuprofen/acetaminophen, stomach medicine and blister care
- Masks for smoky hotpot rooms or poor-air days if desired
- Comfortable footwear is non-negotiable in vertical Chongqing

## Do not bring

- A heavy winter coat
- Oversize luggage that makes regional flights painful
- More than one formal outfit
- A laptop unless work requires it
