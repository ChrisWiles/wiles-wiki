240 Hours · no visa

Every route runs on 240-hour visa-free transit — no L visa. The clock starts 00:00 the day after we enter the mainland, so the arrival day is free. This single rule shapes all five itineraries.

  1. Max ~10 days on the mainland; HK & Macau time doesn't count.
  2. Trip must start in one region and exit to a different one — HK counts, so AUS → mainland → HK → AUS qualifies.
  3. A confirmed onward ticket out within 240 hrs is checked at entry.

03

Our take

Route A

The easy, locked plan

Wuzhen is the trip-maker — nothing else has that slow canal-town night — and the direct PVG → AUS exit saves a day. Best food-and-scenery option with the lowest friction.

Route C

Mountains & lake, fewer trains

Most of Route B without the Xiamen long-leg. Hangzhou + Huangshan get their real time and the TWOV clock gets a comfortable day of buffer. The most "landscape-painting" version.

Route E

Kaylee's Avatar peaks

Zhangjiajie genuinely looks like nowhere else. The two internal flights are a real cost, but it's the payoff. Don't try to bolt it onto another route — the math won't fit.

Route B

The one to avoid

11 hours of train across 5 segments inside a <12-hour TWOV buffer. One slipped train turns into a real problem. Take C — it's most of B — or commit to E.
